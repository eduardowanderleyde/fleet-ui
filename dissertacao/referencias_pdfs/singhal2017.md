## Abstract

The paper presents a system for coordinating autonomous mobile robots in industrial settings,
providing details of implementing a system for managing a fleet of autonomous mobile robots
(AMR) operating in a factory or a warehouse premise. The researchers explored three different
implementation strategies. The first two approaches utilized the distributed computing based
Networked Robotics architecture and communication framework of Robot Operating System (ROS),
while the third employed the Rapyuta Cloud Robotics framework. The study compared these
approaches through simulation as well as real world experiment with actual robots to evaluate
performance. The authors aimed to illuminate the inner working of the Cloud Robotics Platform in
contrast to the usual ROS framework for educational and practical purposes. The research also
identified limitations in the Rapyuta platform and suggested improvements to address them.
