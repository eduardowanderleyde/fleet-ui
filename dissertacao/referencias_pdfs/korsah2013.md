## Abstract

The paper presents a new, comprehensive taxonomy for task allocation in multi-robot systems
that explicitly takes into consideration the issues of interrelated utilities and constraints.
The existing widely used taxonomy for task allocation in multi-robot systems (Gerkey and
Matarić, 2004) was designed for problems with independent tasks and does not deal with
problems with interrelated utilities and constraints, such as task precedence, synergies between
tasks, or scheduling constraints. This work extends that taxonomy to close that gap.
