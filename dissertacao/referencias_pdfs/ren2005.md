## Abstract

This note considers the problem of information consensus among multiple agents in the
presence of limited and unreliable information exchange with dynamically changing interaction
topologies. Both discrete and continuous update schemes are proposed for information
consensus. This note shows that information consensus under dynamically changing interaction
topologies can be achieved asymptotically if the union of the directed interaction graphs have a
spanning tree frequently enough as the system evolves.

Index Terms: Cooperative control, graph theory, information consensus, multiagent systems,
switched systems.
