## Abstract

This review examines the broad roles that communication formats and technologies have
played in enabling multi-robot systems, analyzing both robotic applications requiring
communication and the networking technologies that support them. The authors identify a
significant gap in research addressing joint optimization of robots and their networks together,
and emphasize the growing importance of data-driven and machine learning approaches in
developing communication systems, noting a shift away from manually-designed communication
patterns. The work advocates for a more integrated approach combining robotic algorithms with
networking systems, and concludes by presenting four major open research challenges,
recommending data-driven solutions for addressing them.
